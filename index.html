MY STORY

Every song deserves
a feeling.

I'm Osae-Hienno Philemon Kodua, professionally known as Phil Jazzy, a professional drummer based in Accra, Ghana.

My musical approach is built around pocket, feel, timing and serving the song. Whether behind the drums in church, on stage, at a wedding or in the studio, I believe every performance should communicate something meaningful.

Beyond music, I'm studying Web Application Development at the Ghana Communication Technology University (GCTU). I enjoy balancing creativity in music with innovation in technology.
